Research design

Pre-registered between-subjects experiment, N = 901 U.S. workers.

Three-step belief correction protocol with individualized treatment intensity. 475 treated, 426 control.

The treatment

  1. Explain expert methodology (factual manipulation check: 93.5%).
  2. Elicit subjective risk belief (0–100 percentile).
  3. Reveal expert estimate; display belief gap.

Design property

Both moderators are measured before treatment, so the activation corner is a fixed partition of pre-treatment characteristics rather than an outcome of the manipulation.

The identification problem

Expert trust is observed only in the treated arm; trait anxiety and automation risk are observed in both. Carrying trust into the control arm is what the estimator has to solve.

  1. Proxy.A random forest on 15 pre-treatment terms predicts the 5-pt trust rating.
  2. Principal score.A logit of Truster (trust ≥ 4) on the proxy gives pi for every respondent.
  3. Contrast.Hájek-weighted arm means: pi for Trusters, 1 − pi for mid/low.
Identifying assumption

Identified under randomization plus principal ignorability: pi is a function of pre-treatment covariates only, so the same weights apply in both arms.

Uncertainty from a bootstrap with B = 2000, resampling within arm, with the proxy, the logit and the weights refit in every replicate.
