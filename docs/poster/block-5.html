What this licenses

The Truster results are the ones this design can carry. A null on the mid/low corner increment cannot distinguish a genuinely absent effect from a real one the study lacks the power to see.

Robustness

  • Covariate adjustment (FWL).Truster 0.329, mid/low 0.110, contrast 0.219: the same ordering, modestly attenuated.
  • The cutoff choice does not drive the corner.δ's 95% CI excludes zero at the median, the 58.3rd percentile and the tercile.
  • Nothing is fitted except the treatment effect.Corner membership is a deterministic function of two pre-treatment variables, so no cutoff or sharpness parameter is estimated.

Sensitivity to the identifying assumption

How badly would principal ignorability have to fail?

Driving these estimates to zero requires a hidden differential treatment response of −1.17 SD for the Truster CATE and −0.52 SD for the contrast: large, and opposite in sign to the mechanism under study.
