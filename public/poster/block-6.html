Takeaways & implications

Aggregate effects understate the subgroup that responds.

The two-gate architecture generalizes to any persuasion domain with expert sources and dispositional moderators.

  1. Theoretical.A two-gate model accounts for conflicting prior results as composition across pathways rather than as preference stability.
  2. Methodological.Trust is post-treatment and observed in the treated arm only; a pre-treatment proxy plus principal-score weighting recovers stratum effects from both arms under an explicit, sensitivity-tested assumption.
  3. Empirical.The Truster effect (0.40 SD) is about three times the mid/low one, and among Trusters the high-anxiety × high-risk corner reaches 0.69 SD, roughly 2.4× the effect outside it.
Implication

Risk communication that ignores who trusts the source, and who is personally exposed, will understate responsiveness in exactly the group that responds.

Scope

N = 901 U.S. workers · 62% Democratic including leaners · immediate measurement · trust identified under principal ignorability. Generalizes to climate, vaccines, trade.
